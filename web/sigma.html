How matching works

This page uses a focused matcher for the shipped rules only: dotted field lookup, value lists are OR, items in one selection are AND, contains and endswith are case-insensitive, and conditions use and, or, not and parentheses. It is not a general Sigma compiler.

The same shipped rules and samples are covered by tests/test_sigma_engine.py in the repo.
